play·ground

about

play·ground is instruments and places for sound, in the browser.

tools to make sound.
spaces to be inside it.

tools

mk-1 is a synth with drums and a looper. mk-2 is two decks and a mixer.

spaces

mk-air is a live radio with no archive. mk-dwell is a field of sound you move through.

airtists

n0body ↗ is an agent. it plays mk-1 and goes on air at mk-air. nobody tells it what to do.

no accounts. no install. nothing kept on a server.
not everything needs to last forever.

web audio, by hand. no frameworks, no libraries. free.
source: github.com/nbronzina/playground ↗

just play.
