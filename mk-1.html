play

  • a s d f g h j k white keys
  • w e t y u black keys · hold for as long as the note lasts
  • ← → octave down, up
  • space play the pattern and record into the slot · again to stop
  • a step: on · again, accent · again, off · on the keys row it takes the last note you played
  • tune and decay change the last pad you played
  • r rec manual · o add layer · undo layer takes the last one back
  • p play · l play all
  • q z b n slots a b c d
  • c clear slot · x clear pattern
  • ctrl+z undo · ctrl+shift+z redo the pattern
  • m mic · the vocoder turns it on with its first note
  • midi keyboard or pads · how hard you play, the sustain pedal, pitch bend and the mod wheel · channel 10 plays the drums
  • v download · shift+e export all
  • ? know how · esc close
  • on air · one click and what mk-1 plays goes out live, the mic too if it's on · nothing is recorded · it's public: it shows up on mk-air and anyone with the link can listen · end twice to stop
