## 1. Intent

What this is and what it should feel like when done.
Not what it should do — what it should *feel like*.

## 2. Constraints

Hard constraints that cannot be violated.
Technical AND perceptual — both listed, both non-negotiable.

## 3. Decisions already made

What was decided and why. The *why* is required.
Implementation must not revisit these unless explicitly opened.

## 4. What was deliberately not done

Adjacent approaches that were rejected, with brief rationale.

## 5. Vocabulary

Key terms used in this brief with their Playground-specific meaning.
Do not assume shared vocabulary — define it here.

## 6. Success criterion

How to verify the implementation matches the creative intent.
Must be observable. "It feels right" is not a criterion.

## 7. Do not touch

Specific files, functions, or behaviors that must remain unchanged.

## 8. Resume prompt

One paragraph. Claude Code reads this at session start and confirms
understanding before proceeding. Should contain: module, intent,
constraints, and success criterion in compressed form.
